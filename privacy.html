Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 August 2026

Marquee Reader collects no data about you. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party services of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is no “us” for it to be sent to — we operate no servers.

Most privacy policies are long because the app does a lot with your data. This one is short for the opposite reason. What follows is simply the detail behind the paragraph above.

What we collect

Nothing. Marquee Reader contains no analytics framework, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDKs. We cannot see which feeds you read, how often you open the app, or whether you use it at all.

We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information, and there is no sign-in.

Where your data lives

Your subscriptions, folders, starred articles, settings, and downloaded articles are stored on your own devices.

If you are signed in to iCloud, some of that data also syncs between your Apple TV and the Marquee Reader Companion app through your own private iCloud database, using Apple’s CloudKit. Specifically: your feed subscriptions and their folders, your starred articles, your app settings, and articles you send from the TV to your phone.

This data lives in your personal iCloud account. It is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy, and we have no access to it — a private CloudKit database is readable only by the account that owns it. The developer cannot read it, and neither can anyone else. If you sign out of iCloud, the app keeps working entirely on-device.

What the app connects to

Marquee Reader makes network requests to exactly two kinds of destination:

  • The feeds and websites you have chosen to subscribe to. The app downloads each feed to check for new articles, and downloads images and article content from those sites so you can read them.
  • Apple’s iCloud, for the syncing described above.

That is the complete list. There are no analytics endpoints, no content delivery networks of our own, and no third-party icon, image, or search services.

When the app fetches a feed, that publisher’s server sees the request the same way it would see a web browser — including your IP address and the app’s identifier, which is MarqueeReader/<version> (Apple TV; +https://itr8.tech). We identify the app honestly so publishers know who is requesting their feed. What each publisher does with their own server logs is governed by their privacy policy, not ours.

Site icons

The small icons shown next to each feed are fetched directly from the website that feed belongs to — a site you have already subscribed to. Earlier development versions used a third-party icon service, which would have disclosed your subscription list to a company you never chose. That was removed before release. No third party is told which sites you read.

Children

Marquee Reader is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children. The articles it displays come from the feeds you choose to add, and their content is not under our control.

Your choices

Because no data is collected, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on your behalf — requests of that kind have no counterpart here.

You remain in full control of what the app stores locally: delete a feed or a folder to remove it, or delete the app to remove everything from that device. To remove synced data from iCloud, delete your feeds in the app, or remove Marquee Reader’s data through iCloud settings on your Apple device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date at the top. If a future version of the app ever collects anything, that change will be described here plainly and before the fact — not buried in a release note.
