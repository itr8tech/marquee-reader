Getting started

Adding your first feeds

Open Feeds and choose Add Feed. You can type a website address rather than a feed address — Marquee Reader will look for the feed on that site. If you are starting from scratch, the empty state offers a curated starter list you can load with one press.

Typing without the remote

Entering web addresses with a TV remote is miserable, so install the free Marquee Reader Companion on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Add feeds there, and they appear on the Apple TV. You can also share any page from Safari straight into the companion.

Bringing feeds from another reader

Most RSS readers export an OPML file. In Marquee Reader go to Settings ▸ Import and give it the address of your OPML file to import your whole subscription list at once.

Common questions

Why hasn’t a feed updated?

Marquee Reader checks each feed roughly once an hour by default. This is deliberate: an Apple TV left on the screensaver would otherwise poll publishers around the clock, and independent sites pay for that traffic. You can set a faster or slower schedule per feed in the companion app or on the TV. A manual Refresh always checks straight away.

If a feed says Not responding, its server has been failing for a while and the app is retrying less often. If it says Removed by the publisher, the site has told us the feed is permanently gone, and the only useful fix is to remove it.

My feeds aren’t syncing to my phone

Both devices must be signed in to the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive enabled. Sync is usually within seconds, but can take a couple of minutes. Nothing syncs if you are signed out — the app still works fully on-device.

How do I use the screensaver?

It appears on its own after the TV has been idle for a while (adjustable in Settings, or turn it off entirely). While it is showing:

  • Swipe left or right to page through headlines
  • Hold the centre button to open that article and read it
  • Swipe up to mark everything as read
  • Swipe down to leave the screensaver

You can have it draw from your newest articles or only from your starred ones, show one headline with its picture, and set your own photo as the background.

Can I make the text bigger?

Yes — Settings ▸ Text Size scales the entire interface, not just article text. Marquee Reader also supports VoiceOver throughout, and reads articles from non-English feeds in the appropriate voice.

Is my reading history private?

Entirely. The app collects nothing, contacts no third-party services, and has no accounts. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I need the companion app?

No. Everything can be done on the Apple TV. The companion is free and makes typing far easier, and it is the simplest way to back up your subscriptions — it can export them as an OPML file.

Reporting a problem

The more of this you can include, the faster it gets fixed:

  • What you did, and what happened instead
  • Your Apple TV model and tvOS version (Settings ▸ General ▸ About)
  • The Marquee Reader version (Settings, at the bottom)
  • If it concerns one feed, that feed’s address
